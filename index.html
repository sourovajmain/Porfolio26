BhcJobs — Performance Marketing for Saudi Recruitment.

BhcJobs · Recruitment Marketing

Turning job campaigns into a measurable candidate acquisition funnel

BhcJobs runs recruitment campaigns for Saudi Arabia across multiple job categories. The marketing challenge is not simply generating more leads — it is attracting candidates who match role requirements and giving the operations team enough data to follow up efficiently.

Challenge

High-volume recruitment needs, different eligibility criteria for each role, and the risk of low-quality or irrelevant applications.

Strategy

Role-specific paid campaigns, clear qualification messaging, landing-page alignment, audience refinement, remarketing, and analytics-led optimization.

Measurement

GA4, GTM, Meta Ads Manager, Search Console, Microsoft Clarity, CRM feedback, and campaign-level lead quality review.

  • Built and optimized paid media campaigns around job-specific requirements instead of using one generic recruitment message.
  • Used Meta Ads and Google Ads to support candidate acquisition across recruitment campaigns and job categories.
  • Connected campaign decisions with analytics and on-site behavior through GA4, GTM, Search Console, and Microsoft Clarity.
  • Used creative testing, audience refinement, landing-page messaging, and remarketing to improve campaign relevance.
  • Aligned marketing with candidate follow-up and CRM workflows so lead quality feedback could inform future optimization.
